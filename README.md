# Stocks (paper trading sandbox)

A homemade stock trading simulator: start with fake money, buy and sell against real market prices, and track your gains and losses over time.

> Status: early brainstorming. Nothing is built yet.

## Goals
- Paper trade with a virtual cash balance
- Use real (or near-real) market prices
- Track portfolio value, P&L, and trade history

## Not a goal
- Real brokerage integration or real money. This is a sandbox only, not financial advice.
